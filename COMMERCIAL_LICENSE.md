# Evaluation and commercial licensing

The project's original software is available under the noncommercial
terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Viewing the public repository and
demonstrations does not constitute a commercial implementation license.

Written permission is required before corporate technical evaluation,
internal proof-of-concept or pilot use. Commercial manufacturing,
testing, product development, integration, distribution and deployment
require separate commercial licensing, including internal business
use where no scanner is sold.

For permission or licensing inquiries, contact the repository owner
through [GitHub](https://github.com/CocoHusky). Describe the organization,
intended use, evaluation scope and proposed deployment. No permission
is granted merely by submitting an inquiry.

Commercial pricing, support, warranties and production qualification
are not included in the public license and would require separate
agreement. Third-party software remains available under its own
applicable terms.
